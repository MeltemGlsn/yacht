About Us

Elegant Yachting Tours started with a simple idea: a day on the water should feel easy, personal and unforgettable. We are a small team of skippers, hosts and chefs based in Southampton.

Whether you are looking for a peaceful escape, a celebration with friends or a luxurious day out with your family, we plan every trip around you. You choose the pace, we take care of the rest.

Safety comes first on every tour. All of our crew are fully trained and every yacht is checked before it leaves the marina.
